# Project Governance

AstinusLab is currently maintained by one person, the lead maintainer listed in [Authors](AUTHORS.md). Our goal is to maintain a high-quality, stable, and secure forensic platform.

## Decision Making Process

Major architectural changes, new features, and security fixes are decided by the lead maintainer. Bug reports and feature requests go through the repository's [Issues](https://github.com/FFernandez1/Astinuslab/issues); pull requests from outside contributors are not accepted for 1.0.0 (see `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository root).

### Feedback
Use the repository's [Issues](https://github.com/FFernandez1/Astinuslab/issues) for questions, bugs and ideas. See [Security reporting and support](SECURITY.md) for the private vulnerability route and the best-effort maintenance policy. Keep sensitive reports out of public issues.

### Becoming a Maintainer
Currently, maintainer status is granted by the existing lead maintainer based on consistent, high-quality contributions over time.
