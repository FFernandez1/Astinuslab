# Small workflow measurements — 7 September 2026

Five synthetic scenarios passed once each; no warmup, corrective measurement or
successful-case repeat. These are component measurements, not forensic throughput,
installed lifecycle acceptance, production capacity limits or performance gains.
No product optimization was made.

Source: `2023c7ff14717bc9176b4c8cb39954cd3a0690e8` plus the uncommitted benchmark
files identified below. Measured at 13:44:56–13:45:02 UTC in the isolated

Operation excludes fixture/import setup. UI operation is response delivery through
DOM completion and two animation frames, including handler/paint scheduling.
The generated hash input was likely page-cached; its approximately 1.33 GB/s rate
is not sustained physical-disk performance. RSS sums per-process resident pages,
including shared pages counted in multiple processes, while kernel cgroup memory
accounting charges shared pages differently. The 25 ms RSS samples can miss brief
peaks; both measures are reported without treating them as interchangeable.
